Single booth wall adapted for another medical congress

The elements of a booth's two walls combined into one 225 x 255 cm wall, as a CMYK TIFF for the printer.

Enlarge image:

Wall of 225 x 255 cm with the device in focus: tablet showing the exam, probe in hand, cart and screen grid, and beside it a close-up of the tablet and the probe.

The request

The medical equipment company from an earlier congress booth was exhibiting at another event, with room for a single wall.

What I did

I combined the key elements of the two old walls into a 225 x 255 cm wall: the opening line, the device, a hand holding a tablet with the probe, the cart, a grid of screens and the feature ladder.

The result

Over three rounds on the same day I adjusted the background, the size of the tablet and the cart, the logo position and the grid, and delivered the 6378 x 7228 pixel CMYK TIFF and the print PDF.
