Enlarge image:

Final logo: two interlocking speech bubbles, like links between customer and service provider, with the slogan and the app icon in the brand blue.

The request

The owner of a services app found the old logo unappealing.

What I did

He wanted a logo that explained the app better, keeping the slogan and the blue he already used.

The result

I presented three directions, and the chosen one came as a complete package with vectors and horizontal and compact versions, plus the icon in Play Store and App Store sizes, a favicon and a color and type guide.
