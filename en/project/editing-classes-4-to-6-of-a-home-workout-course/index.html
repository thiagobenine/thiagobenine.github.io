The request

The same client came back with the second stage of the home weight loss course: classes 4, 5 and 6, each with the audio recorded separately.

What I did

I kept the style of the first classes, with an animated text hook in the opening, a preview of each exercise, a ring timer, a water break or active rest and captions with the spoken word highlighted. In class 5, a segment of the 4K file came out corrupted when written to disk, so I scanned all 39 segments, redid only what had errors and checked the entire decoded final file, with zero errors.

The result

The three classes, with 16, 14 and 15 exercises, were delivered in 4K and a lightweight copy, with sync within 0.4 ms and checked at 10 frames per second.
