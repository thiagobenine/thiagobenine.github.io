Home page on desktop: the three perfume families, search by name or brand and filters by audience and category. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

A perfume shop wanted to show its products online and take orders without paying a monthly platform fee.

What I did

I built a catalog with 105 products in 7 categories, an order button that opens WhatsApp with the chosen product and automatic sold-out labels, plus a simple dashboard for the team to update products and stock, with a user guide.

The result

Everything runs on the hosting's free plan.
