Bot that exports conversations from a web CRM

A Chrome extension that saves a CRM's WhatsApp conversations as TXT, JSON and spreadsheets, without changing anything in the system.

The request

An education company wanted a copy of the WhatsApp conversations stored in its web CRM.

What I did

I built a bot as a Chrome extension that only reads the CRM and saves each conversation as TXT and JSON, with CSV spreadsheets. The bot splits the work across computers without repeating conversations and resumes on its own when it stops.

The result

In the full run, it extracted 184 of 184 conversations and 3,701 messages, with no errors and numbers matching the CRM.

What I delivered

  • 01Read-only Chrome extension for the CRM
  • 02Conversations in TXT and JSON
  • 03CSV spreadsheets of conversations and stage changes
  • 04Work split across computers

  • 06Installation and user guide in PDF
