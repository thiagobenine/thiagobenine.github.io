150 mm spoon modeled in code, with a handle of flat facets that twist in a spiral.

The request

A product team needed to turn a spoon sketch into a technical model for molding.

What I did

I modeled a 150 mm spoon with a faceted spiral handle in parametric CAD, within 0.8 mm of the sketch, with a 2.8 mm wall, a bowl 10.4 mm deep and a 3-degree draft on every wall so it releases from the mold.

The result

I delivered version 1.2 in STEP, the STL for an FDM prototype and the technical drawings with dimensions.
