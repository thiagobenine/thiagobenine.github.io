The spreadsheet's simulation tab with a made-up example: item cost, target margin and the minimum and suggested price on each sales channel. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

An activewear brand wanted to sell on marketplaces without losing margin to each channel's fees.

What I did

I built a spreadsheet with formulas that compares the fees of four marketplaces, calculates each item's price to keep the target margin and simulates sales through affiliate creators.

The result

On the TikTok Shop store, I created from scratch a listing for leggings in 7 colors with 21 variants and a size chart, adjusted the variants of 3 other items, added 2 new T-shirts and left 9 products active.
