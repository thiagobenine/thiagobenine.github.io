New homepage banner for a Pix (Brazil's instant payments) discount, on desktop and mobile. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

An online store selling digital games had 94 products listed and wanted to sell more.

What I did

I wrote a 4-page assessment, backed up every product and rewrote 94 titles and 93 descriptions for SEO without touching prices, stock or page URLs, checking everything through the API. I fixed products with separate console versions, the bundle with expansions and the real subscription length, and created 2 homepage banners for desktop and mobile, 5 new icons for the benefits bar and a 10% first-purchase coupon.

The result

I also replaced the 8 homepage testimonials with versions that blur personal data, turned off the old banners without deleting them and left a guide for describing new products.
