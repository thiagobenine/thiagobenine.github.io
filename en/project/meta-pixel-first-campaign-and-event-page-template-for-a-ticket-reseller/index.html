The Meta setup in 5 steps, the 2 pixel events, the event page template with its 5 WhatsApp buttons and the PDF guide to repeat it for every show. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

The ticket reseller that already had its site and video from me wanted to advertise each show on Meta with a page of its own for the event.

What I did

I set up the business portfolio, the page and the ad account, installed the pixel on the site and made every click to WhatsApp count as a contact. I built the event page template on the site, with the venue map, the sections and a green WhatsApp button at the top, published two pages and the first traffic campaign for one of them.

The result

At the end I delivered a click-by-click PDF guide so the team can duplicate the page and the ad for every new event.
