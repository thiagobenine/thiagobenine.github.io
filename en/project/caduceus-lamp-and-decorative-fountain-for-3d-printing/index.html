Caduceus lamp and decorative fountain for 3D printing

A caduceus lamp with a DNA strand and a water fountain with a village, split into parts that fit each printer's build plate.

The request

A brand of 3D printed decor pieces wanted a caduceus lamp and a themed water fountain to sell.

What I did

I started with a 7-page feasibility study and modeled the lamp with stylized snakes, a double helix DNA strand and room for the bulb, refined over two rounds. For the fountain, the second round followed a new direction chosen by the client: a 210 x 200 mm base in a single piece for the filament printer, a pump compartment, channels and rock supports, plus a detailed village in 4 plates for the resin printer.

The result

Everything shipped as watertight STL files, checked to fit each printer's build plate, with images of the parts and a printing guide.
