The request

The same client from the deals bot came back to pull the articles from each edition of a PDF newspaper into an organized format.

What I did

I built an extractor that separates each article with its headline, text and position on the page, joins continuations and links front-page teasers to their articles. Text recognition (OCR) only runs on pages where the text is an image, and ads are left out.

The result

The 4 test editions yielded 505 articles from 240 pages, with a visual proof for each page and 91 automated tests.
