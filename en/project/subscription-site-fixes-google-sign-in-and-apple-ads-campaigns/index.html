The request

The owner of a subscription study website wanted anything broken fixed before advertising.

What I did

I swept 13 pages at three screen widths, found 17 bugs (3 serious, like the mobile menu hiding the plan and account links) and fixed and published all of them with before-and-after screenshots. The Google sign-in screen now shows the site's name and domain, with the brand verified by Google, and audio playback of long texts works again.

The result

Since the client preferred to advertise directly in Apple's App Store, I planned and built four search campaigns for his other app (brand, category, competitors and discovery), which went live with his approval.
