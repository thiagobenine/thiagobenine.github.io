Parents' area: creating the 4-digit passcode, the report for each mission, screen time with a chart and a daily limit, and the reading and accessibility options. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

In the fourth stage of the English app for children, the client wanted to give parents control and information with no internet and no sign-up.

What I did

I built a 4-digit passcode for the parents' area in Flutter, with recovery through a harder math question, and the same lock before any link that leaves the app. The area got a report per mission with correct answers, attempts and words learned, each day's screen time in a bar chart and an optional daily limit that leads to a rest screen only an adult can unlock.

The result

For accessibility I added three text sizes, high contrast, a color-blind mode for the paints and a screen reader review, with 2 signed Android builds, a demo video and the source code.
