Three feed posts: a quote from the law, a short tip and a question with its answer, in green and cream.

The request

The same law firm came back to bring its visual identity to Instagram.

What I did

I created 6 posts, 5 stories, 4 carousels with captions, 18 highlight covers and 3 profile photo versions, with 8 editable files for Canva and the kit's fonts. It came with a 5-page guide on swapping text and photos, creating a new carousel and following Brazil's advertising rules for lawyers.

The result

The kit was approved on the fourth version.
