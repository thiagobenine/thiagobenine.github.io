Snap-fit Christmas house for 3D printing

A decorative Christmas house with a sign, a star and holly as separate parts to print in 2 colors.

Enlarge image:

The house from the front and at three quarters, with the Merry Christmas sign, the star and the holly in red on the white body.

The request

A company wanted a Christmas house of about 8 cm as a custom souvenir, based on a reference image.

What I did

I modeled the house with a flat base, a roof with snow on the eaves and a Merry Christmas sign, a star and holly as separate parts that fit into the body, so the white and the red print apart.

The result

After the fit test printed by the client, version 2 came out larger and closer to the reference, with adjusted fits, and the delivery included STL, 3MF print projects and a PDF guide.
