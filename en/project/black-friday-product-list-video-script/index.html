The first two pages of the script: how to read it, title options, a thumbnail idea, the opening hook and the first product on the list.

The request

A tech product list channel on YouTube asked for a script about what is worth buying on Black Friday.

What I did

I researched 10 products among the most searched and best rated by buyers and wrote the ready-to-record script, with suggestions of what to show on screen, three title options, the thumbnail idea and a final table with the models and links.

The result

In the second version the opening got more direct, the script focused on the everyday benefit and each product leads into the next.
