The request

A robotics company needed to track the quotes it sent and chase customer replies without relying on manual reminders.

What I did

I built a CRM with a 5-stage board that pulls quotes and contacts from the ERP through its API every 15 minutes. After a quote goes out, the system sends the first follow-up in 2 business days and another one 5 business days later, during business hours and from the salesperson's own inbox, and stops on its own when the customer replies, approves or declines.

The result

Next came the feedback request after delivery, automatic archiving, a date filter and stages, users and messages editable from the dashboard.
