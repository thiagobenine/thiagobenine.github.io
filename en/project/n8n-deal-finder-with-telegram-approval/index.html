User guide delivered with the bot: the cover with the four steps and the day-to-day page, part by part. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

The owner of a deals group spent hours hunting for offers by hand.

What I did

I built an n8n workflow that searches for deals on Shopee and Mercado Livre (a large Latin American marketplace) with a minimum discount and category filter. It creates the affiliate link, builds the message in the group's format and sends a card with buttons on Telegram to approve or discard it.

The result

The delivery included an installation and user guide in PDF.
