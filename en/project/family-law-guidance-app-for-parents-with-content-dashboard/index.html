The request

A legal professional wanted an app that explains to parents, in plain language, their rights around custody, child support and visitation.

What I did

I built the app in Flutter with email or Google sign-in, a guide by category, articles with a short summary and FAQs. Content is edited in a web dashboard, where the client creates, renames and reorders categories and writes articles while seeing a phone preview.

The result

I delivered the Android app and an iPhone version that opens in the browser and can be added to the home screen.
