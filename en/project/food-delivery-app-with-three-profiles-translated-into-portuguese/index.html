Translating a delivery app with three profiles into Portuguese

Customer, restaurant and courier apps plus the web dashboards in Portuguese, with test builds for Android.

The request

A delivery app with customer, restaurant and courier profiles was entirely in English and needed to be ready to sell in Brazil.

What I did

I translated the three Flutter apps, with more than 1,700 strings, and the web dashboards, including the sign-in screens and the app names on the phone.

The result

I generated test builds of the three apps for Android through an automation in the repository itself, and delivered the work in separate pull requests for the client to review.
