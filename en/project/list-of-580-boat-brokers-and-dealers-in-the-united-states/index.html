The request

A client in the jet ski business needed a list of boat brokers and dealers in the United States, with business contact details for each one.

What I did

I built the list from a yacht brokers association's directory and a public dealer locator, reading each company's website on public pages only and keeping business contacts only. The final spreadsheet has 580 companies (203 brokers and 377 dealers in 49 states), 373 with email and 575 with phone, plus a tab that explains how to read it.

The result

I checked two samples of 40 rows against each company's website, with 97.5% accuracy, and corrected the method before delivery.
