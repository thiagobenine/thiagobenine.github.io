Screens from Missions 2 and 3: the new character's question, picking who will introduce themselves, the right red apple and the garden with its colors back. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

In the second stage of the English app for children that I'm building in stages, the client wanted the introductions and colors missions to follow the same pattern as the first one.

What I did

I built both missions in Flutter with two new characters drawn in code, screens that show who is speaking, a choice of the character that stands for the child, a name badge and a scene to paint by tapping or dragging, with a pattern on each paint for kids who can't tell colors apart. The stage added 43 new audio clips, between English lines and guidance in Portuguese.

The result

The delivery had 2 signed Android builds, a demo video of each mission and the source code.
