Design system: structural neutrals and the four colors reserved for collaboration statuses, with AA contrast. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

A creator marketing platform for businesses had its product document and needed the design system and first screens in Figma.

What I did

I built 72 components with variants and Auto Layout, 9 text styles and a variables collection, and used them to design 8 desktop screens, 4 mobile screens and 4 illustrated states: empty, loading, error and no permission.

The result

The client approved it without asking for changes, and it was delivered 2 days ahead of schedule.
