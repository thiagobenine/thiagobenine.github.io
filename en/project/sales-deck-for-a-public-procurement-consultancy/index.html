The request

A new public procurement consultancy needed material to start selling.

What I did

I refined the typographic logo from the client's sketch and built the 13-slide sales deck with speaker notes, an 8-page company portfolio to send by phone, support material with a script, answers to objections and outreach texts, and the sales playbook from first contact to signing.

The result

Everything came out in PDF and editable files, also in Google Slides, Google Docs and Canva.
