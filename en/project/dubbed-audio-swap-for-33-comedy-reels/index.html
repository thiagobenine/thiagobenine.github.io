A new dub synced line by line in 33 vertical videos, with leveled volume and the picture untouched.

The request

A client had 33 comedy Reels and a new dub recorded for each one, and needed to replace the original voice with the dubbed one without losing lip sync.

What I did

I fitted the dub line by line in each video, kept the music and ambience underneath, leveled them all to -14 LUFS and kept the picture, duration and frame count identical to the originals, checking each Reel from start to finish before delivery. In the 15 versions the client had adjusted on his own, I put the men's original voices back in 12 and fixed one word in another, and I redid one of the Reels with captions on every line.

The result

The client approved the final version.
