The request

An online program sold on Hotmart, a Brazilian platform for digital courses, had each talk in a separate module and the old offer still on the sales page.

What I did

I merged the 19 single-talk modules into one module for the current year's edition, in the same order, deleted the 18 empty modules, created a hidden module for the next edition and fixed accents in lesson titles.

The result

At checkout, I replaced the offer with the full price plus a launch coupon with an end date, enabled the buyer's phone field, which turns on the abandoned cart reminder via WhatsApp, installments of up to 12 payments and automatic card recovery, and delivered a 7-page final report with the steps to open sales.
