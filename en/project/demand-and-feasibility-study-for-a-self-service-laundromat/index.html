Final research report: the collection funnel, from ad reach to responses, and what would get people to use the laundromat, with preferred days and times. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

An entrepreneur was about to open a self-service laundromat and needed to choose between two locations before signing a contract.

What I did

I created the questionnaire, two online forms with a coupon for respondents, Instagram and Facebook ads within a 2 km radius of each location, artwork with a QR code for neighborhood groups and a study of the competition and the neighborhoods.

The result

In 7 days the ads reached 16,071 people and generated 939 clicks, and the answers from 29 residents went into an 8-page final report with the break-even point, payback period and the recommended location, prices and hours.
