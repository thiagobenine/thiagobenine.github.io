The request

A page that recommends tools and car accessories wanted to know which products to feature and needed the videos ready to post.

What I did

I researched and delivered a list of 10 products in a 14-page PDF and a spreadsheet, and put together a second version when the client swapped 3 items. Then I made 10 vertical Reels of about 17 seconds with licensed footage, brands removed and on-screen text, plus a cover, story and post for each product.

The result

Three Reels got a new version in the revision.
