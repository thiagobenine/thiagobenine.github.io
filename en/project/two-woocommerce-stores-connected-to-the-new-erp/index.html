Two WooCommerce stores connected to the new ERP

The path of each paid order: the plugin on both sites sends the order to the new company's account, under the store of the site it came from, as completed and without generating an invoice on its own. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

The owner of two tire stores started invoicing through a new company and needed the orders from both sites to land in its ERP.

What I did

I imported the 120 products of both stores into the new account with each tire's tax classification, created one store per site in the system and pointed the order plugin on both sites to the new account, with each paid order coming in as completed and without generating an invoice on its own.

The result

At the check, the 17 paid orders from the first days were all in the system, completed, with no invoice generated and none duplicated.
