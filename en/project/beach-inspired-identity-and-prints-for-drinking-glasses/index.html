Identity and prints for a drinking glass brand

The logo and one of the prints applied to a classic ribbed Brazilian glass, in the brand's tropical palette.

The request

A custom drinking glass brand wanted an identity with a beach feel and a Brazilian spirit.

What I did

I presented three logo directions, and the chosen one became the brand, with prints inspired by Tropicalia (the 1960s Brazilian art movement) and the coastline.

The result

I applied the prints to glass mockups and refined the client's idea until it matched what she had in mind.
