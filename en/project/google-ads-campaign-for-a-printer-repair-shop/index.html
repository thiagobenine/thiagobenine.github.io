Google Ads campaign for a printer repair shop

A search campaign with 4 ad groups, 55 keywords and 138 negatives, approved after a policy disapproval.

The request

A printer repair shop wanted to get service calls from people searching for repairs in the area.

What I did

I built a search campaign for 6 nearby cities, during business hours, with 4 ad groups by intent, 55 keywords, 138 negative keywords, 4 responsive search ads, 6 sitelinks, 8 callouts, a structured snippet, and call and WhatsApp assets.

The result

When the ads were disapproved under the third-party technical support policy, I rewrote the headlines and descriptions, and the campaign was approved and went live two days later, with a PDF summary so the client could follow along.
