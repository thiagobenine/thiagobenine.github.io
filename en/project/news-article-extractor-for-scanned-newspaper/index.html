News article extractor for a scanned newspaper

The extractor learned to read a second, image-only newspaper, with front-page teasers linked to their articles.

The request

The same client came back with a second newspaper whose PDF is image only, with no selectable text.

What I did

I switched to text recognition (OCR) on the full page, handled the opinion page and generalized the extractor to serve both newspapers with the same code. The 5 test editions yielded 408 articles, and 60 of the 61 front-page teasers were linked to the right article.

The result

The final version shipped with 188 automated tests.
