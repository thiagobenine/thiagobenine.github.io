The request

A website that creates custom songs wanted ads in a customer testimonial style, showing an order being placed on a phone.

What I did

I wrote the script, approved before production, and edited 2 vertical 30-second videos with the site's screens step by step, first-person narration and word-by-word captions with the spoken word highlighted.

The result

Each version has a different opening for testing, the sung snippet from the preview and the closing call to action with a hand pointing at the button.
