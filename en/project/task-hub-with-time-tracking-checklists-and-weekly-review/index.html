The request

In the second phase of the task hub, the client wanted a checklist and time tracking on each task, a daily summary and a weekly review.

What I did

I gave every task a checklist and time tracking, with an alert when a timer is left running for more than 8 hours, and created the morning summary, with the calendar, urgent items and what was done the day before. The weekly review shows planned versus done and time by area and project, with alerts by priority, and an official list of areas now sorts everything by area, project and task.

The result

Supplier emails are now linked to projects, productivity queries with time estimates were added, and the server now has 30 actions, with the manual updated.
