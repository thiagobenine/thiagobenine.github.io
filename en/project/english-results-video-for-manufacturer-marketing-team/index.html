The request

The regional marketing team of a construction equipment manufacturer needed an internal video in English showing what the team delivers beyond what people usually see.

What I did

I wrote the script in English, revised it with the team's manager over 4 versions and helped choose the data and lines that show the business impact. I built the 1920x1080 video with the company's own footage, English narration and captions, animated icons and charts, a map of the countries served and a mosaic of team photos.

The result

The final cut runs 2 minutes 16 seconds, and the manager approved it.
