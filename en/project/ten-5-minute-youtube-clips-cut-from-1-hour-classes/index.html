Ten 5-minute YouTube clips cut from 1-hour classes

Each class of 47 to 70 min became a clip of about 5 min with its strongest topic block, clean audio and a thumbnail.

A 37-second excerpt from one of the ten horizontal lecture cuts, with slide zooms and chapter captions. The presenter's face and the channel handle were blurred to protect the client's identity.

The request

A psychoanalysis class channel sent 10 recorded classes, 47 to 70 min each, and wanted a clip of about 5 min from each one.

What I did

In each class I picked the strongest topic block, with a beginning, middle, end and hook, removed pauses, mistakes and hesitations, leveled the volume and cleaned up the noise. I designed the clips with the slide next to the camera, zoom on text passages, a lower third with the topic of each passage and an end card with a quote.

The result

I delivered the 10 clips in Full HD with a thumbnail for each, ahead of the agreed deadline.
