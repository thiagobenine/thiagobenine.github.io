New look with three themes and effects for a block puzzle game on Google Play

A block puzzle game got original art in three themes, combo effects and little characters that celebrate a clear board.

The three new block themes: colorful, chocolate and gems. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

The owner of a block puzzle game already on Google Play wanted a modern look in the style of the most popular block games, without copying any of them.

What I did

I created original blocks in three themes (colorful, chocolate and gems) with bevels, shine and tray pieces sized exactly to the board cells, plus line glow, sparks and confetti when lines clear. Combos show an on-screen cheer with a voice line, a crew of little block characters celebrates a clear board, and the home screen icon now matches the store icon.

The result

Before submission, the board logic passed 3,000 simulated games with no errors, and version 2.8 went live with an updated store listing.
