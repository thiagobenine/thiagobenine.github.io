The request

A player sent 42 minutes of a match from a third-person shooter and wanted a dynamic video for YouTube.

What I did

I delivered a 28-minute version plus an alternative in a 1920s newspaper style, which was the one chosen, opening on the strongest play, with an eliminated stamp on every death, headlines on multi-kills and 8 slow-motion replays.

The result

I added 14 sound memes with zoom-ins, stretches sped up 3 to 24 times, captions of what was said, bleeped swear words, a ranking of the top 5 plays and the audio on 3 separate tracks.
