The request

An online store selling truck and bus tires wanted a 30-second video for its home page and for ads.

What I did

I made three versions: a muted horizontal loop for desktop, a vertical one for phones and a narrated one for Google Ads.

The result

I used licensed stock footage, close-ups of the store's own tires and its marketplace seller panel shown without prices, and the video went live on the home page and in the campaign.
