Up to 18 installments, checkout and order sync to the ERP in two tire stores

A pricing plugin with Pix (Brazil's instant payments) and card from 1 to 18 installments, a checkout with a single installment selector and paid orders sent to the ERP automatically.

The request

The owner of two WooCommerce tire stores needed the Pix price and the card price in up to 18 installments to match on the product page and at checkout, and orders weren't reaching the ERP.

What I did

I wrote a custom plugin for each store that calculates the card price from the Pix price and shows the 1 to 18 installment table with the total per tire, and set up the checkout with a single installment selector, Pix with automatic confirmation, a side cart with shipping cost and delivery time by ZIP code and a clean order summary. In the second store, the sessions table had 3,097 rows for 521 sessions and the cart was losing items, so I rebuilt the table to the WooCommerce standard, keeping the newest row of each session.

The result

Finally, another plugin started sending each paid order to the ERP through its API, with a daily retry up to 4 times if it fails and the invoice created as pending in both stores.

  • 03Side cart with shipping and delivery time by ZIP code

  • 05Automatic sending of paid orders with a pending invoice
