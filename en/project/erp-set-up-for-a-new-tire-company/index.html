ERP set up for a new tire company

New Bling account for the new company of a tire store group, with both units, the accountant's tax rules and the records brought over from the sister company.

The request

A group of tire stores opened a new company and needed to invoice through it under the standard tax regime.

What I did

I configured the account from scratch, with the company data, the digital certificate, the units in two states, the operation types copied from the sister company and adjusted to the accountant's rules (single-stage PIS and Cofins and the 4% rule for imported tires) and the numbering continuing from the previous system. I issued test invoices for both units in the sandbox before production, classified the imported tires and migrated the sister company's records without duplicates, with 2,759 contacts and 68 products all checked after the import.

The result

Alongside, I reviewed the tax rules in the system of another company in the group.
