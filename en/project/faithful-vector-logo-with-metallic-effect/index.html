Faithful logo vectorization with a metallic effect

A logo rebuilt in vector from a small image, with a metallic shine and versions for dark and light backgrounds and one color.

The request

A company had its logo only as a 1968 x 661 px image, with no file usable for printing, embroidery or decals.

What I did

I redrew the logo in vector using the image as a template, rebuilt the metallic effects and gradients in layers, identified the slogan font and converted the text to outlines.

The result

The overlay with the original matched between ninety-five and ninety-nine percent in each part, and the package was done in under 10 hours: SVG, PDF in RGB and CMYK, Illustrator, EPS and PNG, in versions for dark and light backgrounds and one color, with a color sheet.
