The automation flow in Make: the daily list pasted into the spreadsheet becomes a WhatsApp reminder with one click, and the updated schedule and the summary by city go out on their own in the daily runs. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

A company that sells in installments in Brazil and a neighboring country was chasing every customer by hand, one message at a time.

What I did

I built a Make automation connected to a Google spreadsheet: the person in charge pastes the day's list exported from the finance system and, with one click, each customer gets a WhatsApp reminder with the installment number and amount, in Portuguese or Spanish and in the local currency. When an installment is marked as paid, the customer receives the updated schedule, and a team member gets a daily summary by city.

The result

There are two WhatsApp numbers, one per country, on the official API without taking the number off the phone, with 333 customers and 1,337 installments already loaded and illustrated user guides.
