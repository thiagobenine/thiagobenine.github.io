An 18-second excerpt of the vertical e-book ad, with animated captions, here with only the music and sound effects.

The request

In the second traffic stage for the same e-book, the client wanted the creatives and the campaign ready to run.

What I did

I made 4 image ads for feed and stories with the book in a mockup, the copy for each ad and 2 vertical 27-second videos with a female voice-over, word-by-word captions and pages of the e-book on screen.

The result

I set up the sales campaign with one ad set for teachers and another for remarketing, 6 ads with tracked links, and published it only after the client's written approval.
