The request

A pizzeria wanted customers to build their pizza on a food delivery app by picking size, crust and flavors, without touching the menu that was already selling.

What I did

I created a build-your-own pizza category in 3 sizes (25 cm with up to 2 flavors, 35 cm with up to 3 and 45 cm with up to 4), with the crust first, 38 savory and 12 sweet flavors with a per-flavor surcharge and ingredient descriptions for all of them, plus a combos category at the top with 5 combos. Everything was built paused and went live only after the owner approved it from screenshots, with nothing deleted.

The result

Along the way I found a panel bug that zeroed the surcharges whenever a product or combo was saved, restored the values, checked every combo in the app and delivered a PDF guide so he can add items on his own.
