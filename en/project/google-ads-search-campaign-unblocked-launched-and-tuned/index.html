Search campaign rebuilt with the correct URL and ready to publish, with the lead form goal and bidding to maximize clicks.

The request

A custom software company had its first search campaign stuck in draft because of an error that kept it from running.

What I did

I traced the cause to an old URL that no longer loaded, rebuilt the campaign with the correct URL and made the website form submission the only primary conversion, tested in GA4.

The result

After the first clicks, I switched the keywords to phrase match, added 15 negative keywords, removed an old conversion action and started tracking taps on the WhatsApp button as well, with the test from a phone confirmed.
