Home page of one of the stores on desktop, before and after: performance score from 76 to 89 in PageSpeed, with the largest element on screen appearing in 1.0 s. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

After the server move, both stores still had low PageSpeed scores, with the largest page element taking 6 to 19 s to appear on mobile.

What I did

I loaded the stylesheets not needed for the first screen without blocking rendering, removed the preloads that competed with the main product photo, made the product video load only on tap and lightened the mobile banner, and one tire page dropped from 14 MB to 2.5 MB. On desktop the home page scores went from 87 to 92 and from 76 to 89, and on mobile from 57 to 60 and from 51 to 56, always with checkout tested through Pix and card.

The result

The same project also added a review card on the home page, the revised price box, abandoned cart recovery in both stores and videos on the product pages.
