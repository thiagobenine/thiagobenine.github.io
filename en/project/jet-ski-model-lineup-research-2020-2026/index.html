The request

A client needed the jet ski model lineups of three manufacturers, year by year from 2020 to 2026, from official sources only.

What I did

I built a spreadsheet with 347 rows, one per model and year, with the standardized official name, family, status (new, continued or discontinued) and the source for each row, plus a timeline tab. Anything without official confirmation went into a separate tab, with 7 items, so it wouldn't mix with the confirmed data.

The result

I delivered it the same day in Excel and CSV, and the client approved it right away.
