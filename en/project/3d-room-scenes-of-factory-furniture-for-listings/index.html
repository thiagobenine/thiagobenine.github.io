3D room scenes of factory furniture for product listings

Three 3D rendered scenes with the furniture modeled to the spec sheet measurements: a nursery, a restaurant and a party room.

The request

A sales representative for furniture factories needed to show the products in a real setting in the main images of the listings.

What I did

I modeled each piece to the measurements and finish on the spec sheet and built the whole room in 3D, with light, contact shadows and reflections coming from the render itself, with no photo pasted on top. There were three rooms: a compact apartment nursery with a wardrobe, crib and dresser, a restaurant with four table and chair sets, and the shared party room of a residential building, a common amenity in Brazilian apartment complexes.

The result

The nursery went through several rounds until it matched the angle of the reference provided and was delivered in high resolution, at 3840 x 2880 pixels.
