The request

After the sales site, the brokerage wanted its corporate site to match, but it was full of spam, with broken links and pages showing critical errors.

What I did

I made a full backup, removed 372 spam posts and 1,317 spam comments, updated the plugins, replaced the ones that broke pages with native features and locked down old files that were open to anyone. I built 8 new pages in the sales site's look, with a new header and footer, published at the same URLs, and set up 15 redirects from the old pages.

The result

The 13 forms that sent visitors to another site now show a submission confirmation, and a crawl of the whole site found zero broken internal links.
