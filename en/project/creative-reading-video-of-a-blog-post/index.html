Three frames from the vertical version: the helmet, the gauntlet and the wall of breastplates, with the animated caption in Montserrat and the key word in gold italics. The caption text is replaced with neutral placeholder text, keeping font, size and position.

The request

An emotional wellness professional wanted to bring his blog posts to social media and picked one of them as a first test.

What I did

I wrote a reading script faithful to the text and edited the video in vertical and horizontal, with animated captions highlighting key lines, scenes that follow the idea of the text and a licensed piano track. In the revision round, a mature male narration replaced the voice recorded on a phone, new scenes rounded off the reasoning of the text, and a scene requested by the client was added.

The result

The final video runs just over 1 minute and went out with an end card and a tagged link to measure visits to the blog, and the client approved the final cut.
