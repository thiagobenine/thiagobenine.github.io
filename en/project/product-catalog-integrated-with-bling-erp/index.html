Product catalog connected to the Bling ERP, with prices and stock always up to date

The store's catalog now pulls products, photos, prices and stock from the Bling ERP on its own, with no double entry.

How the catalog was connected to Bling: a read-only private app, sync every hour, on visits and from a panel button, and the storefront already showing the photo that came from the system (names, labels and prices covered). Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

The natural products store already had a catalog with ordering through WhatsApp, but every change to a product, price or stock level in the management system had to be repeated by hand.

What I did

I connected the catalog to Bling, a Brazilian ERP, through a read-only private app: the sync runs every hour, every 15 minutes when someone visits, and from a button in the admin panel, with photos converted to WebP, new products added automatically, out-of-stock items removed from the storefront, and sale prices that survive each update. In the same delivery, the catalog moved to new hosting with its own domain and an SSL certificate, and the password recovery email started working again.

The result

Of the 744 products, 742 went live, and 10 were checked side by side against the ERP.
