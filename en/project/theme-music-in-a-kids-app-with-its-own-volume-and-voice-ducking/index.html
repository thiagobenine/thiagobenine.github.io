The 3 music excerpts on a time scale, the volume that dips under the narration, the opening with skip and mute, and the parents' controls. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

In the English app for children that I build in stages, the client received a 47-second theme song and wanted it on the opening screen, the map and the star reward without getting in the way of the voice lines.

What I did

I cut the excerpts with smooth fade-ins and fade-outs and wrote a custom player that lowers the music whenever there is narration and brings it back afterwards. The opening has skip and mute buttons from the very first frame, and the parents' area got a music switch and a separate volume, both saved on the device.

The result

I delivered 2 signed Android builds, the demo video, the code and step-by-step notes for swapping the music.
