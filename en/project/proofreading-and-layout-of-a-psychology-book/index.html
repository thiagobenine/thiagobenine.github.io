Pages from the laid-out interior: a part opener with the paper and envelope background the author asked for, a chapter opener and a body page, with tuned hyphenation and justification. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

A psychologist was publishing her first book and sent the text in Word, about 63 thousand words.

What I did

I proofread it with 100 corrections and 76 suggestions tracked in the file itself and laid out the interior in two formats, 14 x 21 cm for a print-on-demand publisher and 5.5 x 8.5 inches for Amazon, with the part openers redone from her artwork.

The result

I also delivered the covers for both platforms, with flaps and a calculated spine, and a validated EPUB e-book, and followed the publication through on both stores.
