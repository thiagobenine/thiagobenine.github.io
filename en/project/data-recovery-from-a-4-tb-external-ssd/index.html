Data recovery from a 4 TB external SSD that stopped opening

The starting point: Disk Management showing the 4 TB SSD as not initialized and PowerShell confirming the disk with no recognized partition (original screens in Brazilian Portuguese).

The request

The client's 4 TB external SSD stopped opening, and Windows started showing the whole disk as not initialized and unallocated.

What I did

I ran the diagnosis through remote access, read-only, and a full scan of the disk found the original volume with 259,259 files.

The result

Following my step-by-step guidance, the client extracted everything to a new 5 TB hard drive, with a guided format of the SSD at the end, and confirmed it worked.
