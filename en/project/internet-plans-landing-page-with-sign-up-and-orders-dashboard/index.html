The request

An authorized reseller for an internet provider needed a website to sell the plans and organize installation requests.

What I did

I built a landing page with the plans and benefits, a quick inquiry pop-up and a full sign-up form, with a validated taxpayer ID, ZIP code and privacy consent, plus a password-protected dashboard to change prices and see each order, with a copy-data button and a spreadsheet export. I published it on free hosting, with an email alert for each order and the Google Ads conversion tag on the WhatsApp click, and later added an email field, a direct link to the sign-up form, business fields and a PDF guide for changing prices.

The result

There were 5 orders on the first day and 197 orders in two weeks, most of them from Google Ads.
