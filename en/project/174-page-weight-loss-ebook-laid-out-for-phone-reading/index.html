Three pages in phone format: a chapter opener with a photo, an intensity table and a self-assessment with fillable fields. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

An author had 78 pages of text and tables on weight loss and strength training, without a single image, and wanted an e-book to sell.

What I did

I created the visual style with 3 cover options and sample pages for approval, and laid out the 10 chapters in a format designed for reading on a phone, with licensed stock photos, tables that don't break across pages, 551 fillable fields and a clickable table of contents.

The result

The e-book came to 174 pages, with the editable InDesign package checked page by page against the PDF.
