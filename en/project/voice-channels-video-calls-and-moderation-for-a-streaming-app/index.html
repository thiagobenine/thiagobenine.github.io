The request

The owner of a gaming live streaming platform wanted viewers to talk over voice and video inside the app.

What I did

Across seven stages I built voice channels for up to 15 people, one-on-one calls and video in both, with 720p cameras and native video calling on iPhone. Live streams gained moderators who can mute and ban in the chat, plus a way to send the audience to another live stream when one ends, with a notice at the destination, and the admin team got a separate panel with its own login and per-person permissions, along with a finance panel.

The result

Every part was tested on the server and on screen before reaching the stores, and version 1.7.4 was published on the App Store and Google Play.
