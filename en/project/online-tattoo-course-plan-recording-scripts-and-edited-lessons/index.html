The request

A tattoo artist had the outline of an online course on fine line and mini realism and needed to turn it into recorded lessons ready to sell.

What I did

I organized her outline into a plan of 6 modules and 14 lessons, wrote the script for each lesson and a guide to record everything in one day with a phone, covering camera settings, shots, light, sound, framing and a checklist, plus support material on biosafety. With the footage in hand, I edited the 5 pilot lessons with cuts, captions, titles, sped up passages, an opening and a closing.

The result

I also built the course members area on the sales platform, with the modules and lessons published.
