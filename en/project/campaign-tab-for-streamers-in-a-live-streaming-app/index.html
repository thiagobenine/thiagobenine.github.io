Campaign tab for streamers to track hours and goals in the app

New tab in a live streaming app shows each streamer their hours, monthly goal and predicted level in the campaign.

The Campaign tab on the phone in three situations: goal in progress, goal reached and a month with no goal, with the current and the projected level.

The request

The gaming live streaming platform already tracked campaigns in its admin panel, but streamers could not see their own progress.

What I did

I built the Campaign tab inside the profile settings, shown only to streamers enrolled in a campaign, with hours and monthly average, what is left to reach the goal, current and predicted level, the level table and previous months. Each streamer sees only their own numbers, gets a one-time notification when their enrollment is approved and, with a zero goal, sees a no-goal notice.

The result

Before release it passed 46 database checks and 23 function tests, with screens checked at 390 and 320 px, and the tab reached the stores in version 1.6 for iPhone and Android.
