The request

The client had the STEP file of a lamp clamp designed for injection molding and needed mechanical adjustments without changing the design.

What I did

I reinforced the arm, fitted the cavity to the socket with a dimensioned interference fit and merged the two halves of the shade into a single solid. The part's shape, size and volume stayed the same.

The result

I delivered the STEP file with renders and a PDF of instructions, even over the weekend.
