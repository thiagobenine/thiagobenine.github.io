The group's landing page on mobile and the group creative for feed and stories. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

An affiliate marketer who shares product deals had no Facebook page and wasn't seeing results from organic posts.

What I did

I created the page, the Business Portfolio, the ad account and the Pixel, a lightweight landing page with a button to the WhatsApp group, the group kit with name, description, welcome message and rules, and creatives for the group and a pilot product in feed and stories formats, and also turned her Christmas artwork and 3 of her creatives into ads. I launched 2 campaigns on a low daily budget, one for group sign-ups and one for traffic to the product on Shopee.

The result

By the end of the project there were 71 clicks to Shopee at a very low cost each, and 6 group sign-ups.
