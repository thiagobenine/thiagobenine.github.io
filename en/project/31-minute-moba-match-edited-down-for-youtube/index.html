Pieces of the match edit: the triple kill banner with the score, the teammate's death counter, the slow-motion replay and the end card (client deliverable in Brazilian Portuguese).

The request

A gaming content creator sent the recording of a 31-minute match, with their webcam in the corner, and asked me to highlight every time a teammate died.

What I did

I cut it to 8 min 52 s in 54 blocks, with a three-part hook, a title card, a counter for the 12 deaths with zoom and a sound effect, kill banners with the score, 27 captions of the creator's lines with the webcam enlarged and a slow-motion replay of the triple kill.

The result

I closed with the creator's subscribe segment in the middle, an end card with the score and licensed music, and in the second version I lowered the music, shortened the opening and delivered the thumbnail with only the character.
