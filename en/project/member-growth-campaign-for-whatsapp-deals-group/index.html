The request

The owner of a deals group, the same client from the deals automation project, wanted to bring more people into his WhatsApp group.

What I did

I created a landing page with 5 categories, sample cards and a click counter by traffic source. I also made 10 graphics for the feed, Stories and WhatsApp Status, a 15-day promotion plan, a Status calendar, a Stories kit and a short Reel.
