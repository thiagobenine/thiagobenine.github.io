The request

An innovation consultancy wanted to sell its four products on social media.

What I did

I wrote the plan for the four products and made the pieces for the first campaign, with three feed and story ads, a 7-screen carousel and three posts, adjusted with the client until approval, and prepared the sales page with payment by link, a WhatsApp button and the schedule open only to those who paid.

The result

I launched and monitored the campaigns in Ads Manager, changing creatives, audience and the destination to conversations, and over the month they reached 5,110 people and started 37 conversations.
