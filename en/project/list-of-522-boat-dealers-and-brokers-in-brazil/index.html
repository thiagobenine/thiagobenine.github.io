The 522 companies by state, from most to fewest, split between dealers and brokers, with where each email came from and how many have a phone, a website and a social profile. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

After the United States list, the same boating client wanted a picture of the Brazilian market, with a business contact for each company.

What I did

I crossed Brazil's open company registry data, filtered by the boat retail activity code and by boating names, with the dealer pages of two shipyards and the list of a brokers' association, and reached 522 companies (32 brokers and 490 dealers) in 25 states and the Federal District. Each email comes from the company's website or, with no website, from its registry entry, flagged as such, with no accountant or personal emails, and every email domain was checked (24 were dropped).

The result

I checked two samples of 40 rows and delivered Excel and CSV files with summary and how-to-read tabs.
