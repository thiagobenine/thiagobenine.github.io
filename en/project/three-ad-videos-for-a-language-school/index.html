The request

A language school needed 3 videos of about 1 minute for paid ads on Instagram and Facebook, one for each audience, from footage of an actress.

What I did

I edited all 3 with the hook in the first line, word-by-word captions in the brand colors, animated notebook pages and an end card with the free assessment button pointing to the ad's button, in 9:16 and 4:5. In the revision round, the marketing team chose among 3 backgrounds and 3 caption styles, and I replaced the white wall with the brand's blue and a notebook grid, cleaned up the audio of one of the recordings and redid the actress's cutout around her hair and hands.

The result

The client approved the 6 videos for the campaign.
