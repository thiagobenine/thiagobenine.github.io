Overnight monitoring of digital newspaper editions

Six nights checking the digital newsstand of 3 newspapers to find out when each page and each article becomes available.

Five nights of checks on three titles: when each edition shows up with only the pages and when it is complete, with the text of the articles. Labels translated for this English version; the original is in Portuguese.

The request

For the newspaper article extractor, the client needed to know when the digital editions become ready to read.

What I did

Using a subscriber account, I followed the digital newsstand of 3 newspapers for 6 nights, every hour and then every 30 minutes between midnight and 7 AM, without bypassing any of the platform's restrictions. Page images show up between 1:00 and 2:30 AM, but the text is only released between about 4 and 7 AM, and 108 of 113 articles matched the same page in the PDF.

The result

I delivered spreadsheets of the articles by time, with the pages still lacking text at 4:30, 5:00 and 5:30 AM ready for OCR, a consolidated summary of the test days and the extractor with the adjustments.
