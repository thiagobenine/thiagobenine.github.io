The store logo and the three carousel banners: combat sports gear, agility training at home and shipping across Brazil.

The request

The client wanted to sell combat sports and agility gear without holding inventory.

What I did

I created the store on Shopee (a major online marketplace in Brazil) and integrated it with Dropi (a dropshipping supplier platform), with a logo, brand guidelines, three banners and the storefront layout. I added categories and products with titles optimized for Shopee search, bulleted descriptions and a glove size guide.

The result

I delivered editable artwork so the client could carry on alone.
