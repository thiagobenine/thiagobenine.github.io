Box, sleeves and tags for a home fragrance line

A room spray box with a brushstroke pattern, three sleeves for cream uncoated paper and three liquid soap tags, ready for print.

Enlarge image:

Home spray box of 7 x 7 x 20 cm in the three fragrances of the line: brushstroke print on a brown background and a cream band with each name.

The request

A home fragrance brand whose labels I had already designed needed packaging for the room spray and the pieces that go with the line.

What I did

I designed the 7 x 7 x 20 cm box on the printer's template, with a brushstroke pattern that suggests a bird on a brown background, a discreet logo and a manifesto on the back, refined until approval.

The result

I also made 3 type-only sleeves of 34 x 4 cm for cream uncoated paper and 3 double-sided 7 x 10 cm tags with a die line, all as print PDFs with bleed and outlined fonts.
