The request

The client from the teaching e-book came back for a new volume in the collection, this time with English activities for parents and children.

What I did

From his draft I laid out the 20 A4 pages with a new front and back cover, bright colors, word cards, drawing space, a mini dictionary and stock children's illustrations with a commercial license.

The result

I delivered the light PDF with a clickable table of contents and bookmarks, the mockups and the open InDesign package with fonts and images.
