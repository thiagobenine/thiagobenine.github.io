Discord server for the orders of a roleplay shop

Commercial server built from scratch in under a day, with roles, channels, ticket orders and a catalog.

The request

A crafts business inside a Western-themed roleplay server needed its own server to take and organize orders.

What I did

In under a day I built from scratch 13 roles, 6 categories and 29 channels with permissions, plus a 5-question order form that opens a private channel for each order. The team claims the order, marks the received, in production, ready and delivered stages, and when it is closed with a reason the conversation is saved in the log channel.

The result

Later I added partner establishments up to 31, each with its own role and private channel, along with catalog cards for 18 products, rules, a welcome message and a pinned management guide.
