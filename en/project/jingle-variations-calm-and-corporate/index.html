The request

A client had a jingle with a Caribbean rhythm and wanted two variations of it to play under a voiceover.

What I did

I transcribed the melody and harmony of the original and rewrote the arrangement note by note in two versions: a calm one with piano, strings and cello, and a corporate one with guitars, bass and light percussion, in the same key and at almost the same length. During revisions, the calm version got a slower intro that speeds up to the original tempo, the corporate version was trimmed to guitars only, and the airy string layer came out of the background.

The result

Each version was delivered as a 24-bit WAV, a 320 kbps MP3 and MIDI, mixed to -16 LUFS with room left for the voice.

What I delivered

  • 01Calm version with piano, strings and cello
  • 02Corporate version with guitars
  • 03Arrangements written note by note from the original
  • 04Slow intro that speeds up to the original tempo
  • 05WAV, MP3 and MIDI for each version
  • 06Loudness at -16 LUFS with room for a voiceover
