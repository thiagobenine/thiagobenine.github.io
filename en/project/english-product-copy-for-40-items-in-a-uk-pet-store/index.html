English copy for 40 products in a UK pet store

The request

A pet products dropshipping store aimed at the United Kingdom needed English sales copy for 40 products.

What I did

I wrote the name, description, Google title and meta description for each one, removing claims that couldn't be backed up, and started with 3 products to approve the tone.

The result

I applied everything directly in the store editor, with a copy of the original texts saved before making any change.
