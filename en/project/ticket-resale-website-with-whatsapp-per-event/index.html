The home page already on its own domain: menu with events, how to buy, about us and stores, and WhatsApp support front and center (brand and company registration number left out; client deliverable in Brazilian Portuguese).

The request

A reseller of tickets for concerts and sports events sold through Instagram and wanted a website that built trust and took every interested buyer straight to WhatsApp.

What I did

I built the site on Wix, mobile first, with event cards (artwork, date, venue and sections), a WhatsApp button that opens the chat with the event name already filled in, a step-by-step guide on how to buy with the company video, an about section with team photos, the two physical stores with maps, and the company details with a public registry lookup.

The result

I registered and connected the custom domain with an SSL certificate and handed over a two and a half minute narrated video tutorial so the team can swap events on their own.
