Pricing strategy in 3 pages: the launch price, when to raise it and where to hold back.

The request

The same supplement brand came back to set the selling price on each channel before launch.

What I did

I built an 11-tab spreadsheet with the cost per unit and per 3-pack kit, the fees and shipping tiers of Mercado Livre's classic listing, a 4% safety margin and an analysis of 263 competitor listings. The plan launches at 10% off for 2 months, returns to full price in the third month and opens the second channel in the fourth, and shows that the kit nets 1.9 to 2.7 times more per sale.

The result

I also calculated that 262 orders a month cover the fixed costs and summarized everything in a 3-page PDF.
