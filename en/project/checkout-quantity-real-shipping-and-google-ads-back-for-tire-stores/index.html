Editable checkout quantity, real delivery times and Google ads reinstated in two tire stores

Order summary with minus, plus and remove, installments that follow the quantity, shipping calculated by carrier and the Google campaign back online.

The request

After the first stage, the client wanted to change the quantity right at checkout, offer shipping with real delivery times and advertise again, because Google had blocked the ads, flagging the site as compromised.

What I did

I added minus, plus and remove buttons to the order summary in both stores, with a tap area of at least 40 px on mobile, and made the installment table follow the quantity, with more than 151,000 values checked and no difference between the page and the server. In the second store, with a backup, I fixed broken shipping tables and a ghost zone and connected carrier-based shipping, with real delivery times by state instead of a flat fee per tire.

The result

I filed 3 appeals and a support case with Google, which confirmed there was no malware, and the campaign was running again the next morning, with the 11 sitelinks fixed and purchase tracking audited.
